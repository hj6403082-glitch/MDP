## 6. Units, provenance, detection limits, quality flags

- Non-standard columns carrying metadata: **none**
- **Measurement units are NOT stored.** OpenAQ v3 returns a unit per sensor (e.g. µg/m³; CO is frequently mg/m³ for Indian CPCB stations), but the collector discarded it. Pooling CO across stations with mixed mg/m³ and µg/m³ would be a ~1000× error. **Units are unverified and unharmonised.**
- No detection limits, instrument/provider identifiers, or QA/QC flags are present. Below-detection values cannot be distinguished from true lows.
- **Weather provenance:** temperature/humidity/wind/pressure come from the **Open-Meteo reanalysis (modelled)**, not station instruments, and were attached by nearest ~0.1° grid cell. They are modelled covariates, not co-located observations.
